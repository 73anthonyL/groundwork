# ELECTRICAL SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

This Electrical Subcontract Agreement (the "Subcontract") is entered into as of February 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between **Mesa Verde Contractors, Inc.**, a California corporation holding California Contractor's License No. 884712 (Class B), with its principal office at 4410 Camino Real Business Park, Suite 200, Camarillo, California 93012 ("Contractor"), and **Lumen Electric Corp.**, a California corporation holding California Contractor's License No. 1098234 (Class C-10), with its principal office at 812 East Santa Clara Street, Ventura, California 93001 ("Subcontractor").

Contractor has entered into a prime contract (the "Prime Contract") with **Ventura County Credit Union** ("Owner") for the construction project known as the **Oxnard Branch Remodel**, located at 1650 Saviers Road, Oxnard, California 93033 (the "Project").

Contractor wishes to engage Subcontractor to perform the electrical portion of the Project, and Subcontractor wishes to perform that work, on the terms stated below. The parties therefore agree as follows.

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Subcontract Work" means all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, tools, transportation, permits, testing, and services required to complete the electrical scope described in Article 3, together with all work reasonably inferable from the Subcontract Documents as necessary to produce the intended result.

1.2 "Subcontract Documents" means the documents listed in Article 2.

1.3 "Final Completion of the Subcontract Work" means the point at which three conditions have been met. First, all Subcontract Work is complete, including all punch list items. Second, all final inspections by the City of Oxnard Building and Safety Division have been passed. Third, Subcontractor has delivered all closeout submittals required by Article 12 and Specification Section 01 77 00.

1.4 "Application for Payment" means a written request for payment submitted by Subcontractor under Article 5.

1.5 "Backcharge" means any cost, expense, or loss incurred by Contractor that this Subcontract makes Subcontractor's responsibility.

1.6 "Architect" means Coastline Design Studio, LLP, the design professional retained by Owner for the Project.

## ARTICLE 2 — SUBCONTRACT DOCUMENTS

2.1 The Subcontract Documents consist of the following:
- (a) this Subcontract, including the Exhibit — Schedule of Values;
- (b) the Prime Contract, to the extent it applies to the Subcontract Work;
- (c) the Project Manual dated November 14, 2025, including Divisions 01, 26, 27, and 28;
- (d) the Drawings listed on Sheet G-001, Revision 2, dated December 8, 2025;
- (e) Addenda Nos. 1 through 3; and
- (f) written modifications issued under Article 10.

2.2 Subcontractor assumes toward Contractor all obligations that Contractor assumes toward Owner under the Prime Contract, to the extent those obligations apply to the Subcontract Work. Contractor will make a copy of the Prime Contract available for Subcontractor's review on request. Pricing information in the Prime Contract may be redacted.

2.3 If the Subcontract Documents conflict, the more stringent requirement governs. Subcontractor shall promptly bring any conflict to Contractor's attention in writing before proceeding with the affected work.

## ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF SUBCONTRACT WORK

3.1 Subcontractor shall furnish and install the complete electrical system for the Project, as shown and specified in the Subcontract Documents. The scope includes the following work.
- (a) Temporary power for the Project. This includes distribution to Contractor's trailer and to trade work areas.
- (b) Safe-off and demolition of existing electrical systems within the remodel area.
- (c) Modifications to the existing 800-amp service, together with a new main switchboard, panelboards, and transformers, under Specification Sections 26 24 13 and 26 24 16.
- (d) Branch circuitry, wiring devices, and grounding, under Specification Sections 26 05 19, 26 05 26, and 26 27 26.
- (e) Interior and exterior lighting fixtures and lighting controls, under Specification Sections 26 09 23 and 26 51 00.
- (f) Conduit pathways, back boxes, and pull strings for data, telephone, and security systems, under Specification Section 27 05 28.
- (g) Dedicated power for ATMs, teller cash recyclers, vault equipment, and the night depository.
- (h) Power and monitoring connections to the fire alarm system, under Specification Section 28 31 11.
- (i) Testing, labeling, and as-built documentation for all of the work listed above.

3.2 Subcontractor shall obtain and pay for all electrical permits and inspections required for the Subcontract Work.

3.3 The following items are excluded from the Subcontract Work:
- (a) data cabling and terminations;
- (b) security system devices and programming;
- (c) fire alarm devices and programming; and
- (d) furnishing of owner-supplied equipment.

Subcontractor shall nonetheless receive, set, and connect owner-supplied electrical equipment as shown on the Drawings.

## ARTICLE 4 — SUBCONTRACT SUM

4.1 Contractor shall pay Subcontractor, for full and complete performance of the Subcontract Work, the sum of **Three Hundred Eighty-Five Thousand Dollars ($385,000.00)** (the "Subcontract Sum"). The Subcontract Sum is subject to additions and deductions made under Article 10.

4.2 The Subcontract Sum includes the following:
- (a) all applicable sales and use taxes;
- (b) all permit and inspection fees;
- (c) all insurance costs; and
- (d) all overhead and profit.

4.3 The Exhibit — Schedule of Values allocates the Subcontract Sum among the principal portions of the Subcontract Work. It will be used to evaluate Applications for Payment. Subcontractor may not reallocate amounts among line items without Contractor's written approval.

## ARTICLE 5 — APPLICATIONS FOR PAYMENT

5.1 Subcontractor shall submit Applications for Payment to Contractor's project manager. Each Application for Payment must cover Subcontract Work performed during the preceding monthly billing period.

5.2 Each Application for Payment must be prepared on Contractor's standard continuation-sheet format and must contain all of the following:
- (a) the Project name, the Subcontract number, and the billing period covered;
- (b) for each Schedule of Values line item, the scheduled value, the value of work completed in previous periods, the value of work completed in the current period, the total completed to date, and the percentage complete;
- (c) a listing of approved change orders, with amounts, shown as separate line items;
- (d) the total amount earned to date, less retainage under Article 6, less amounts previously paid, showing the net amount requested;
- (e) the conditional waivers and releases required by Article 7;
- (f) certified payroll or labor-hour summaries if Contractor or Owner requests them; and
- (g) a signed certification by an officer of Subcontractor. The certification must state that the work covered by the application has been performed in accordance with the Subcontract Documents. It must also state that all amounts previously paid to Subcontractor have been applied to pay laborers, suppliers, and lower-tier subcontractors for the Subcontract Work.

5.3 Contractor may reject or adjust an Application for Payment in three circumstances:
- (a) the application is incomplete;
- (b) the application overstates the work completed; or
- (c) the application is not accompanied by the required waivers.

Contractor shall identify the deficiency to Subcontractor. Subcontractor shall submit a corrected application.

5.4 Payment will be made only for Subcontract Work actually installed at the Project site. **Contractor will not pay for materials or equipment stored off site, or for materials or equipment delivered to the site but not yet incorporated into the Subcontract Work.**

5.5 Payment of an Application for Payment does not constitute acceptance of defective or nonconforming work.

## ARTICLE 6 — RETAINAGE

6.1 **Contractor shall retain five percent (5%) of the amount earned under each Application for Payment** as retainage.

6.2 Retainage held under this Subcontract will be released only after both of the following have occurred:
- (a) Final Completion of the Subcontract Work; and
- (b) Contractor's receipt of retention from Owner.

6.3 **Contractor shall release retainage to Subcontractor within ten (10) days after Final Completion of the Subcontract Work and Contractor's receipt of retention from Owner.** This obligation is subject to Subcontractor's delivery of the waivers and releases required by Article 7, and to any withholding permitted by Article 8.

## ARTICLE 7 — LIEN WAIVERS AND RELEASES

7.1 **With each Application for Payment, Subcontractor shall deliver a conditional waiver and release on progress payment, covering the amount requested, before Contractor makes the progress payment.**

7.2 **After receiving each progress payment, Subcontractor shall deliver an unconditional waiver and release on progress payment covering the amount actually received.** Subcontractor shall deliver this waiver with its next Application for Payment, or earlier if Contractor requests it.

7.3 Subcontractor shall provide conditional and unconditional waivers and releases on final payment in connection with final payment and the release of retainage.

7.4 **All waivers and releases furnished under this Subcontract must be on the statutory forms prescribed by California Civil Code Sections 8132, 8134, 8136, and 8138**, as applicable, completed in full and signed by an authorized representative.

7.5 **Subcontractor shall obtain and deliver to Contractor corresponding conditional and unconditional waivers and releases from each of its lower-tier subcontractors and material suppliers** who have furnished labor, materials, or equipment for the Subcontract Work during the period covered. These waivers must also be on the statutory forms identified in Section 7.4.

7.6 Subcontractor shall keep the Project free of mechanics liens, stop payment notices, and payment bond claims arising from the Subcontract Work. If such a lien, notice, or claim is filed, Subcontractor shall promptly cause it to be released or bonded. If Subcontractor does not do so, Contractor may obtain the release, and its cost will be charged to Subcontractor as a Backcharge.

## ARTICLE 8 — BACKCHARGES, SETOFF AND WITHHOLDING

8.1 **Contractor may set off against any amount otherwise due to Subcontractor under this Subcontract the amount of any Backcharge.** Backcharges include the following:
- (a) costs to correct, complete, or clean up the Subcontract Work;
- (b) costs to repair damage caused by Subcontractor to the work of others; and
- (c) costs resulting from Subcontractor's failure to perform any obligation under this Subcontract.

8.2 Contractor shall give Subcontractor written notice of each Backcharge. The notice must describe the condition and the basis of the charge. Where practicable, Contractor shall give Subcontractor a reasonable opportunity to correct the condition before Contractor incurs the cost.

8.3 **Contractor may withhold from any payment an amount sufficient to cover the cost of correcting Subcontract Work that is defective or does not conform to the Subcontract Documents.** The withholding may continue until the defective work has been corrected and accepted.

8.4 Contractor may also withhold amounts reasonably necessary to protect against the following:
- (a) unreleased liens, stop payment notices, or bond claims arising from the Subcontract Work;
- (b) Subcontractor's failure to pay its lower-tier subcontractors, suppliers, or workers; and
- (c) any amount Owner withholds from Contractor on account of the Subcontract Work.

8.5 Once the reason for a withholding has been removed, the withheld amount, less any Backcharge actually incurred, will be included in the next payment to Subcontractor.

## ARTICLE 9 — PERFORMANCE OF THE WORK

9.1 Subcontractor shall perform the Subcontract Work in a good and workmanlike manner. The work shall comply with the Subcontract Documents, the California Electrical Code as adopted by the City of Oxnard, and all applicable laws.

9.2 Subcontractor shall perform the Subcontract Work in accordance with Contractor's project schedule, as updated from time to time. Subcontractor shall furnish sufficient labor, materials, and supervision to maintain that schedule. Subcontractor shall attend coordination meetings when Contractor requests.

9.3 The Project is an occupied financial institution. Subcontractor shall comply with Owner's phasing, security, and after-hours work requirements described in Specification Section 01 14 00. The following work must be coordinated in advance with Contractor's superintendent:
- (a) any interruption of power to the occupied areas; and
- (b) any work affecting the vault, teller line, or ATM vestibule.

9.4 Subcontractor shall designate a full-time foreman holding a valid California General Electrician certification. The foreman must be on site whenever Subcontract Work is in progress. Contractor must approve any replacement of the foreman.

9.5 Subcontractor shall prepare and submit shop drawings, product data, and samples as required by Division 26. Subcontractor shall not order or install materials that require submittal until the submittal has been reviewed by the Architect.

9.6 Subcontractor shall keep its work areas clean and shall remove its debris on a daily basis.

## ARTICLE 10 — CHANGES

10.1 Contractor may, without invalidating this Subcontract, order changes in the Subcontract Work. These changes may consist of additions, deletions, or other revisions. The Subcontract Sum and schedule will be adjusted accordingly.

10.2 Subcontractor shall not perform any extra or changed work without a written change order or written field directive signed by Contractor. Work performed without such authorization will be considered part of the original Subcontract Work.

10.3 Subcontractor shall submit a written change proposal for each change. The proposal must itemize labor, material, equipment, and lower-tier costs. Subcontractor's markup for overhead and profit is limited to ten percent (10%) on its own work and five percent (5%) on the work of its lower-tier subcontractors.

10.4 If Subcontractor believes that any direction, condition, or act of Contractor entitles Subcontractor to additional compensation or time, Subcontractor shall give written notice of the claim within seven (7) days after the event giving rise to it. Claims not noticed within that period are waived, to the extent permitted by law.

10.5 Where a change also affects Contractor's rights against Owner, Subcontractor's entitlement is limited to the adjustment Owner grants for the Subcontract Work. Subcontractor's entitlement is reduced by Contractor's reasonable markup and costs. This limitation does not apply to changes caused solely by Contractor.

## ARTICLE 11 — INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

11.1 Before starting work, Subcontractor shall furnish certificates and endorsements showing the following insurance, which shall be maintained until Final Completion of the Subcontract Work:
- (a) commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate, including products and completed operations coverage;
- (b) business automobile liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 combined single limit;
- (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by California law, together with employer's liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000; and
- (d) umbrella or excess liability insurance of not less than $3,000,000.

11.2 Subcontractor's general liability and automobile policies shall name Contractor, Owner, and Architect as additional insureds. The additional insured coverage must be primary and non-contributory, and must be provided by endorsements equivalent to forms CG 20 10 and CG 20 37. Each policy shall include a waiver of subrogation in favor of the additional insureds.

11.3 To the fullest extent permitted by law, including California Civil Code Section 2782.05, Subcontractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Contractor, Owner, and Architect, and their officers and employees, from claims, damages, losses, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees. This obligation applies to the extent such claims, damages, losses, and expenses arise out of the negligence, recklessness, or willful misconduct of Subcontractor or of anyone for whose acts Subcontractor is responsible, in connection with the Subcontract Work.

11.4 Subcontractor's indemnity obligation does not extend to claims arising from the active negligence or willful misconduct of an indemnified party, or from defects in design furnished by those parties.

## ARTICLE 12 — WARRANTY AND CLOSEOUT

12.1 Subcontractor warrants that all materials and equipment furnished under this Subcontract will be new and of the quality specified. Subcontractor also warrants that the Subcontract Work will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for one (1) year after Owner's acceptance of the Project, or for any longer period required by the Subcontract Documents. Subcontractor shall promptly correct any defect reported during the warranty period at no cost to Contractor or Owner.

12.2 Subcontractor shall assign to Owner all manufacturers' warranties on equipment furnished under this Subcontract.

12.3 Closeout submittals required for Final Completion of the Subcontract Work include the following:
- (a) as-built drawings showing actual conduit routing and panel locations;
- (b) typed panel directories;
- (c) operation and maintenance manuals;
- (d) test reports for switchboard torque, insulation resistance, and ground resistance;
- (e) the lighting controls acceptance certificates required by California Title 24, Part 6; and
- (f) warranty documents.

## ARTICLE 13 — SAFETY, LICENSING AND COMPLIANCE

13.1 Subcontractor shall comply with Contractor's site safety program and with all Cal/OSHA regulations. Subcontractor shall maintain and enforce its own Injury and Illness Prevention Program. Energized work shall be performed only as permitted by Cal/OSHA electrical safety orders and NFPA 70E.

13.2 Subcontractor represents that it holds and will maintain an active California C-10 Electrical Contractor license in good standing throughout the performance of the Subcontract Work. Subcontractor shall ensure that all workers performing electrical work hold the certifications required by California Labor Code Section 108.

13.3 Subcontractor shall comply with Owner's background-check and badging requirements for personnel working within the secured areas of the branch.

## ARTICLE 14 — DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

14.1 If Subcontractor commits any of the following defaults, Contractor may give Subcontractor written notice to cure:
- (a) Subcontractor fails to perform the Subcontract Work in accordance with the Subcontract Documents;
- (b) Subcontractor fails to maintain the schedule;
- (c) Subcontractor fails to pay its workers or suppliers; or
- (d) Subcontractor otherwise breaches this Subcontract.

14.2 If Subcontractor does not begin to cure the default within forty-eight (48) hours after the notice and diligently complete the cure, Contractor may take either of the following actions:
- (a) supplement Subcontractor's forces; or
- (b) terminate this Subcontract for default and complete the Subcontract Work by other means.

The cost of supplementing or completing the work, including reasonable overhead, will be charged against the unpaid balance of the Subcontract Sum. Subcontractor shall pay any excess to Contractor.

14.3 Contractor may terminate this Subcontract for convenience by written notice. In that event, Subcontractor shall be paid for the following, less amounts previously paid:
- (a) Subcontract Work properly performed through the termination date;
- (b) reasonable demobilization costs; and
- (c) reasonable overhead and profit on the work performed.

Subcontractor shall not be paid anticipated profit on unperformed work.

14.4 If Owner terminates the Prime Contract, Contractor may terminate this Subcontract on the same basis. Subcontractor's recovery will be limited to the amount Contractor recovers from Owner on account of the Subcontract Work.

## ARTICLE 15 — DISPUTES AND GOVERNING LAW

15.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Subcontract through negotiation between their senior representatives.

15.2 If a dispute involves Owner's acts or omissions, or the Prime Contract, Subcontractor agrees to be bound by the dispute resolution procedures of the Prime Contract. Contractor will present Subcontractor's claim to Owner in Contractor's name, at Subcontractor's expense.

15.3 Any other dispute that is not resolved by negotiation shall be submitted to non-binding mediation in Ventura County before litigation is commenced.

15.4 **This Subcontract is governed by the laws of the State of California.** Venue for any action lies in the Superior Court of California, County of Ventura.

15.5 Subcontractor shall continue performing the Subcontract Work while any dispute is pending.

## ARTICLE 16 — MISCELLANEOUS

16.1 Subcontractor may not assign this Subcontract, or sublet any portion of the Subcontract Work, without Contractor's prior written consent.

16.2 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices must be sent to the addresses stated in the preamble, or to another address a party designates by notice.

16.3 This Subcontract, together with the other Subcontract Documents, is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Subcontract Work. It supersedes all prior proposals and negotiations. Subcontractor's proposal is not part of this Subcontract unless it is expressly listed in Article 2.

16.4 This Subcontract may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

16.5 If any provision of this Subcontract is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The unenforceable provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

16.6 Contractor's waiver of any breach is not a waiver of any other breach.

16.7 This Subcontract may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original.
