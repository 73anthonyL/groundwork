# FIRE PROTECTION SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

This Fire Protection Subcontract Agreement (the "Subcontract") is made and entered into as of February 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Northgate Builders Group, a California corporation with its principal office at 4415 Riverpoint Parkway, Suite 300, Sacramento, California 95834, California Contractor's License No. 1048862 (Class B) ("Contractor"), and Allied Fire Protection, Inc., a California corporation with its principal office at 917 Kiernan Industrial Court, Modesto, California 95356, California Contractor's License No. 873319 (Class C-16) ("Subcontractor").

Contractor has entered into a prime contract dated December 15, 2025 (the "Prime Contract") with Coastal Logistics REIT ("Owner") for construction of the project known as Port of Stockton Warehouse 7, located at 1840 West Wharf Road, Stockton, California 95203 (the "Project"). Contractor wishes to engage Subcontractor to perform the fire protection work for the Project, and Subcontractor wishes to perform that work, on the terms stated below.

In consideration of the mutual promises in this Subcontract, Contractor and Subcontractor agree as follows.

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Subcontract Work" means all labor, materials, equipment, engineering, permits, testing, and services required to furnish and install the fire protection systems described in Article 3 and the Subcontract Documents, together with all work reasonably inferable from them.

1.2 "Subcontract Documents" means the documents listed in Article 2.

1.3 "Subcontract Sum" means the amount stated in Article 4, as adjusted by Change Orders issued under Article 10.

1.4 "Application for Payment" means Subcontractor's monthly request for payment submitted in accordance with Article 6.

1.5 "Final Completion" means the point at which the Subcontract Work is fully complete in accordance with the Subcontract Documents, all inspections and acceptance tests required by the authority having jurisdiction have been passed, and all closeout submittals required by Article 12 have been delivered to and accepted by Contractor.

1.6 "Retainage" means the portion of each progress payment held by Contractor under Article 7.

1.7 "Defective Work" means any portion of the Subcontract Work that does not conform to the Subcontract Documents, applicable codes, or the approved shop drawings, or that has been rejected by Owner, the Architect, or the authority having jurisdiction.

## ARTICLE 2 — SUBCONTRACT DOCUMENTS

2.1 The Subcontract Documents consist of: (a) this Subcontract, including its Exhibit; (b) the Prime Contract, to the extent it applies to the Subcontract Work; (c) the Project Manual for Port of Stockton Warehouse 7 dated November 7, 2025, including Division 21 (Fire Suppression) and Sections 01 31 00, 01 33 00, 01 45 00, and 01 77 00; (d) the Drawings listed on Sheet G-001, Revision 3, dated November 7, 2025, including Sheets FP-001 through FP-402; (e) Addenda Nos. 1 through 3; and (f) Change Orders issued after the Effective Date.

2.2 Subcontractor assumes toward Contractor all obligations that Contractor assumes toward Owner under the Prime Contract, to the extent those obligations apply to the Subcontract Work. Contractor shall make a copy of the Prime Contract, with pricing information redacted, available to Subcontractor on request.

2.3 If the Subcontract Documents conflict, the more stringent requirement governs unless Contractor directs otherwise in writing. Subcontractor shall promptly report to Contractor in writing any conflict, error, or omission it discovers.

## ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF SUBCONTRACT WORK

3.1 Subcontractor shall furnish and install complete, code-compliant fire protection systems for the Project, including without limitation:

(a) underground fire service mains from the point of connection five feet outside the building line, including thrust restraint, lead-ins, and riser assemblies;

(b) early suppression fast response (ESFR) sprinkler protection throughout the warehouse storage area designed for the commodity classification and storage heights shown on Sheet FP-101;

(c) wet-pipe sprinkler protection for office, restroom, and dock-support areas;

(d) a horizontal split-case electric fire pump, controller, jockey pump, and test header, sized per the approved hydraulic calculations;

(e) Class I standpipe and hose valve connections at the locations shown;

(f) fire department connections, backflow prevention assemblies, control valves, and supervisory switches, with wiring terminations by others;

(g) seismic bracing, hangers, and supports designed by a California-licensed engineer; and

(h) hydraulic calculations, shop drawings, product data, and permit submittals.

3.2 Subcontractor shall design and install the systems in accordance with NFPA 13, NFPA 14, NFPA 20, and NFPA 24 as adopted and amended by the 2025 California Building Code and California Fire Code, the requirements of the City of Stockton Fire Prevention Division, the Port of Stockton, and Owner's property insurer.

3.3 The following are excluded from the Subcontract Work: fire alarm system design and wiring; electrical power to the fire pump controller; patching and painting of finished surfaces; and fire extinguishers and cabinets.

## ARTICLE 4 — SUBCONTRACT SUM

4.1 Contractor shall pay Subcontractor for full performance of the Subcontract Work the sum of Three Hundred Eighty-Five Thousand Dollars ($385,000.00), subject to additions and reductions by Change Order under Article 10.

4.2 The Subcontract Sum includes all applicable sales and use taxes, permit and plan review fees, engineering fees, and costs of testing required by the Subcontract Documents.

4.3 The Subcontract Sum is allocated as shown in the Exhibit — Schedule of Values. The Schedule of Values shall be used as the basis for reviewing Applications for Payment and may not be revised without Contractor's written approval.

## ARTICLE 5 — SCHEDULE AND TIME OF PERFORMANCE

5.1 Subcontractor shall begin the Subcontract Work upon Contractor's written notice to proceed and shall perform it in accordance with Contractor's construction schedule, as updated from time to time. Time is of the essence of this Subcontract.

5.2 Subcontractor shall submit hydraulic calculations and shop drawings to Contractor within twenty-one (21) days after the Effective Date, and shall submit them to the authority having jurisdiction within five (5) days after Contractor's review.

5.3 Subcontractor shall furnish sufficient labor, supervision, and materials to maintain the schedule, and shall coordinate its work with the structural steel, electrical, mechanical, and roofing trades, including participation in Contractor's building information modeling coordination meetings.

5.4 If Subcontractor is delayed by causes beyond its control and without its fault, Subcontractor shall give written notice to Contractor within seven (7) days after the start of the delay. Subcontractor's sole remedy for such delay shall be an extension of time equal to the delay actually suffered, unless and to the extent Contractor recovers additional compensation from Owner for that delay on Subcontractor's behalf.

## ARTICLE 6 — PROGRESS PAYMENTS

6.1 On or before the twentieth (20th) day of each month, Subcontractor shall submit to Contractor an Application for Payment for Subcontract Work performed through the end of that month, in the format required by Contractor and itemized according to the Schedule of Values.

6.2 Each Application for Payment shall be accompanied by the lien waivers required by Article 8, certified payroll records where required by law, and any other supporting documentation reasonably requested by Contractor or Owner.

6.3 Contractor shall include Subcontractor's approved Application for Payment in Contractor's next payment application to Owner.

6.4 Contractor shall pay Subcontractor the amount approved for each Application for Payment, less Retainage under Article 7 and any amount withheld under Article 9, within seven (7) days after Contractor receives payment from Owner for that Subcontract Work. Contractor and Subcontractor expressly agree in writing to this payment timing, which is consistent with California Business and Professions Code Section 7108.5.

6.5 The provision in Section 6.4 governs the timing of payment to Subcontractor. It is not a condition precedent to payment, and Contractor's obligation to pay for Subcontract Work properly performed is not extinguished if Owner fails to pay for reasons not attributable to Subcontractor.

6.6 Contractor will not make payment for materials or equipment stored on the Project site or off site. Subcontractor may include materials and equipment in an Application for Payment only after they have been incorporated into the Subcontract Work.

6.7 No payment by Contractor constitutes acceptance of any part of the Subcontract Work that is not in accordance with the Subcontract Documents.

## ARTICLE 7 — RETAINAGE

7.1 Contractor shall retain five percent (5%) of the amount approved for each progress payment as Retainage.

7.2 Contractor shall release the Retainage to Subcontractor upon Final Completion of the Subcontract Work and Contractor's receipt of retention from Owner. Contractor shall pay the released Retainage to Subcontractor within ten (10) days after both of those conditions have been satisfied.

7.3 As a condition of release of Retainage, Subcontractor shall deliver the final waivers required by Article 8 and the closeout submittals required by Article 12.

## ARTICLE 8 — LIEN WAIVERS AND RELEASES

8.1 With each Application for Payment, Subcontractor shall deliver to Contractor a conditional waiver and release on progress payment covering the amount requested.

8.2 Within five (5) days after receipt of each progress payment, Subcontractor shall deliver to Contractor an unconditional waiver and release on progress payment covering the amount received.

8.3 With its final Application for Payment, Subcontractor shall deliver a conditional waiver and release on final payment, and within five (5) days after receipt of final payment shall deliver an unconditional waiver and release on final payment.

8.4 Every waiver and release delivered under this Article shall be in the statutory form prescribed by California Civil Code Sections 8132, 8134, 8136, and 8138, as applicable. Contractor will not accept a waiver and release in any other form.

8.5 Subcontractor shall obtain and deliver to Contractor, with each Application for Payment and with each subsequent unconditional waiver, corresponding conditional and unconditional waivers and releases from each of its lower-tier subcontractors and material suppliers whose work or materials are included in the payment. These lower-tier waivers shall also be in the statutory form described in Section 8.4.

8.6 Subcontractor shall promptly pay its lower-tier subcontractors and suppliers in accordance with California law and shall keep the Project free of liens, stop payment notices, and claims arising from the Subcontract Work.

## ARTICLE 9 — WITHHOLDING FOR DEFECTIVE WORK

9.1 If Contractor notifies Subcontractor in writing of Defective Work, Subcontractor shall begin correction within three (3) working days after the notice and shall diligently complete correction.

9.2 If Subcontractor does not remedy Defective Work within the time stated in Section 9.1, Contractor may withhold from any payment otherwise due to Subcontractor an amount reasonably necessary to protect Contractor against the Defective Work that has not been remedied.

9.3 The amount withheld under this Article shall be reasonable in relation to the Defective Work and, where required by California Civil Code Section 8814, shall not exceed one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the estimated value of the remedial work in dispute.

9.4 Contractor shall identify in writing the Defective Work on which any withholding is based and the amount withheld.

9.5 When Subcontractor has remedied the Defective Work and Contractor has verified the correction, Contractor shall release the withheld amount with the next payment due to Subcontractor.

## ARTICLE 10 — CHANGES IN THE SUBCONTRACT WORK

10.1 Contractor may, without invalidating this Subcontract, order additions to, omissions from, or other changes in the Subcontract Work by written Change Order or written Construction Change Directive. Subcontractor shall proceed promptly with changed work once directed in writing.

10.2 Subcontractor shall submit an itemized proposal for any change within seven (7) days after Contractor's request, showing labor hours, labor rates, material quantities and costs, equipment, and markup. Markup for overhead and profit on Subcontractor's own work shall not exceed ten percent (10%), and markup on lower-tier subcontracted work shall not exceed five percent (5%).

10.3 Subcontractor shall not be paid for extra work performed without a written Change Order or Construction Change Directive signed by Contractor.

10.4 If Subcontractor believes any instruction, interpretation, or condition entitles it to an adjustment in the Subcontract Sum or time, it shall give Contractor written notice within seven (7) days after first becoming aware of it, and shall submit supporting documentation within twenty-one (21) days. Claims not timely noticed are waived to the extent Contractor is prejudiced by the lack of notice.

10.5 For changes that arise from Owner-directed work, the adjustment to the Subcontract Sum shall be consistent with the adjustment Owner grants to Contractor for that work, to the extent it relates to the Subcontract Work.

## ARTICLE 11 — INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

11.1 Before beginning the Subcontract Work, Subcontractor shall furnish certificates of insurance and endorsements evidencing the following coverage, maintained until Final Completion and, for completed operations coverage, for at least three (3) years thereafter:

(a) commercial general liability on an occurrence basis with limits of $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 general aggregate, including products-completed operations;

(b) business automobile liability with a combined single limit of $1,000,000;

(c) workers' compensation as required by California law, and employer's liability of $1,000,000 per accident; and

(d) umbrella or excess liability of $5,000,000 per occurrence.

11.2 Contractor, Owner, and the Port of Stockton shall be named as additional insureds on the general liability, automobile, and umbrella policies on a primary and non-contributory basis, and all policies shall include a waiver of subrogation in favor of those parties.

11.3 To the fullest extent permitted by law, and subject to California Civil Code Section 2782, Subcontractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Contractor and Owner, and their officers, employees, and agents, from claims, damages, losses, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or resulting from performance of the Subcontract Work, but only to the extent caused by the negligence, recklessness, or willful misconduct of Subcontractor, its lower-tier subcontractors, or anyone for whose acts they are responsible. Subcontractor has no obligation under this Section for claims arising from the active negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or Owner.

## ARTICLE 12 — WARRANTY, TESTING, AND CLOSEOUT

12.1 Subcontractor warrants that the Subcontract Work will be new, of good quality, free from defects in materials and workmanship, and in conformance with the Subcontract Documents.

12.2 Subcontractor shall correct, at its own expense, any Subcontract Work found to be defective within one (1) year after the date of Owner's acceptance of the Project, or within any longer period required by the Subcontract Documents or by a manufacturer's warranty. Subcontractor shall begin correction within five (5) days after notice, or within twenty-four (24) hours in the case of an impairment to a life safety system.

12.3 Subcontractor shall perform all flushing, hydrostatic testing, main drain tests, fire pump acceptance testing, and alarm and supervisory device testing required by the Subcontract Documents and the authority having jurisdiction, and shall give Contractor at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice of each test.

12.4 Before Final Completion, Subcontractor shall deliver to Contractor: (a) Contractor's Material and Test Certificates for aboveground and underground piping; (b) the fire pump acceptance test report; (c) as-built drawings and final hydraulic calculations in electronic format; (d) operation and maintenance manuals; (e) a spare sprinkler cabinet with heads and wrenches as required by NFPA 13; and (f) written warranties.

12.5 Subcontractor shall provide not less than four (4) hours of training to Owner's facility personnel on operation of the fire pump and sprinkler systems.

## ARTICLE 13 — SAFETY, SITE CONDITIONS, AND COMPLIANCE

13.1 Subcontractor shall comply with Contractor's site-specific safety plan, Cal/OSHA regulations, and Port of Stockton security and access requirements. Subcontractor shall designate a competent person on site at all times its forces are working.

13.2 Subcontractor shall maintain a written fire watch and hot work permit program for all welding, cutting, and brazing operations, and shall coordinate any impairment of an active fire protection system with Contractor at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance.

13.3 Subcontractor shall keep its work areas reasonably clean and shall remove its debris daily to the containers Contractor provides.

13.4 Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable laws, including California prevailing wage requirements under Labor Code Section 1720 et seq. to the extent the Project is subject to them, and shall maintain registration with the Department of Industrial Relations where required.

13.5 Subcontractor represents that it holds, and shall maintain throughout performance, a valid California C-16 Fire Protection Contractor license, and that it is qualified to design and install the systems described in Article 3.

## ARTICLE 14 — SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION

14.1 Contractor may suspend the Subcontract Work in whole or in part by written notice. If the suspension is not caused by Subcontractor, the Subcontract Sum and time shall be equitably adjusted to the extent Owner adjusts the Prime Contract for the same suspension.

14.2 If Subcontractor repeatedly fails to supply adequate labor or materials, fails to make prompt payment to its lower-tier parties, disregards applicable laws, or otherwise materially breaches this Subcontract, Contractor may give written notice of default. If Subcontractor fails to begin curing the default within three (3) working days after receipt of the notice and diligently complete the cure, Contractor may terminate this Subcontract by further written notice.

14.3 Contractor may terminate this Subcontract for convenience by written notice if Owner terminates the Prime Contract or otherwise for Contractor's convenience. In that event, Subcontractor shall be paid for Subcontract Work properly performed through the termination date, together with reasonable demobilization costs, to the extent Contractor recovers those amounts from Owner, but shall not be entitled to anticipated profit on unperformed work.

14.4 Upon any termination, Subcontractor shall deliver to Contractor all shop drawings, calculations, permits, and warranties related to the Subcontract Work performed to date.

## ARTICLE 15 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

15.1 Contractor and Subcontractor shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between their senior representatives within fourteen (14) days after written notice of the dispute.

15.2 Disputes that involve Owner's acts or omissions, or that are subject to dispute resolution under the Prime Contract, shall be resolved in the same proceeding and manner as disputes under the Prime Contract, and Subcontractor agrees to be bound by the outcome to the same extent Contractor is bound.

15.3 Any dispute not resolved under Sections 15.1 or 15.2 shall be submitted to mediation in San Joaquin County, California, and, if unresolved within sixty (60) days, may be brought in the Superior Court of California for the County of San Joaquin.

15.4 Pending resolution of any dispute, Subcontractor shall continue diligent performance of the Subcontract Work, and Contractor shall continue to make payments not in dispute in accordance with this Subcontract.

## ARTICLE 16 — GOVERNING LAW AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

16.1 This Subcontract is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

16.2 Subcontractor shall not assign this Subcontract or subcontract the whole of the Subcontract Work without Contractor's prior written consent.

16.3 Notices under this Subcontract shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses stated in the preamble or to another address a party designates in writing.

16.4 This Subcontract, including the Subcontract Documents, is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Subcontract Work and supersedes all prior proposals and negotiations. It may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

16.5 If any provision of this Subcontract is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force, and the unenforceable provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

16.6 This Subcontract may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.
