# ELECTRICAL SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT

This Electrical Subcontract Agreement (the "Subcontract") is entered into as of February 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Mesa Verde Contractors, Inc., a California corporation, California Contractors State License Board License No. 1048827, Class B, with its principal office at 2250 Gonzales Road, Suite 300, Oxnard, California 93036 ("Contractor"), and Lumen Electric Corp., a California corporation, California Contractors State License Board License No. 1073914, Class C-10, with its principal office at 418 East Channel Islands Boulevard, Port Hueneme, California 93041 ("Subcontractor").

Contractor has entered into a prime contract dated December 15, 2025 (the "Prime Contract") with Ventura County Credit Union ("Owner") for the construction of the project known as the Oxnard Branch Remodel, located at 1790 East Vineyard Avenue, Oxnard, California 93036 (the "Project"). Contractor wishes to engage Subcontractor to perform the electrical portion of the Project, and Subcontractor wishes to accept that engagement on the terms stated in this Subcontract.

In consideration of the mutual promises below, Contractor and Subcontractor agree as follows.

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Architect" means Harbor Line Architecture, LLP, or any successor design professional appointed by Owner for the Project.

1.2 "Contract Documents" means the documents listed in Article 2, as they may be amended or supplemented in accordance with this Subcontract.

1.3 "Final Completion" means the stage at which all Subcontract Work, including all punch list items, testing, commissioning, training, and closeout submittals, has been completed and accepted by Contractor, Architect, and Owner, and all governmental inspections and sign-offs applicable to the Subcontract Work have been obtained.

1.4 "Site" means the Project premises and any adjacent areas that Contractor designates for staging, storage, or access.

1.5 "Subcontract Sum" means the amount stated in Article 13, as adjusted by Change Orders issued under Article 7.

1.6 "Subcontract Work" or "Work" means all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, tools, transportation, permits, testing, and services required to complete the electrical scope described in Article 3 and the Contract Documents.

1.7 "Substantial Completion" has the meaning given in the Prime Contract.

1.8 "Lower-Tier Party" means any sub-subcontractor, supplier, materialman, equipment lessor, or other person or entity of any tier that furnishes labor, materials, or equipment to or through Subcontractor for the Work.

## ARTICLE 2 — CONTRACT DOCUMENTS

2.1 The Contract Documents consist of: (a) this Subcontract, including its Exhibit; (b) the Prime Contract, including its general, supplementary, and special conditions, to the extent they apply to the Subcontract Work; (c) the Project drawings prepared by Architect and its electrical engineer, Coastline MEP Engineers, Inc., dated November 20, 2025, sheets E-0.1 through E-6.4; (d) the Project Manual dated November 20, 2025, including without limitation Specification Sections 26 05 00 (Common Work Results for Electrical), 26 05 19 (Low-Voltage Electrical Power Conductors and Cables), 26 05 26 (Grounding and Bonding), 26 24 16 (Panelboards), 26 27 26 (Wiring Devices), 26 51 13 (Interior Lighting Fixtures), 26 09 23 (Lighting Control Devices), 27 05 28 (Pathways for Communications Systems), and 28 46 21 (Fire Alarm Modifications); (e) all addenda issued before the Effective Date; and (f) all Change Orders and written directives issued after the Effective Date.

2.2 Subcontractor acknowledges that it has received, read, and understood the Contract Documents. To the extent the Prime Contract imposes obligations on Contractor with respect to the Subcontract Work, Subcontractor assumes those obligations toward Contractor in the same manner and to the same extent that Contractor has assumed them toward Owner. Contractor shall have the same rights and remedies against Subcontractor that Owner has against Contractor under the Prime Contract, to the extent applicable to the Subcontract Work.

2.3 If the Contract Documents conflict, the more stringent requirement, the greater quantity, or the higher quality shall govern, unless Contractor directs otherwise in writing. Subcontractor shall promptly report any conflict, error, or omission it discovers and shall not proceed with affected Work until it receives direction from Contractor.

2.4 Copies of the Prime Contract, with pricing information redacted, are available for review at Contractor's office during normal business hours.

## ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF WORK

3.1 Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete the electrical Work for the Project in strict accordance with the Contract Documents, including without limitation:

(a) safe-off and demolition of existing electrical systems within the remodel area, including removal of abandoned conductors to their source;

(b) replacement of the existing main switchboard and two distribution panels, with new feeders, as shown on sheets E-3.1 and E-3.2;

(c) all branch circuit wiring, devices, boxes, and conduit for teller line, office, lobby, break room, and restroom areas;

(d) furnishing and installing all interior and exterior lighting fixtures, lighting controls, occupancy sensors, and emergency egress lighting;

(e) dedicated power to the ATM enclosure, vault alarm panel, security camera head-end, and network equipment rack;

(f) empty conduit, pull strings, backboxes, and sleeves for low-voltage, data, and security systems furnished by others;

(g) modifications to the existing fire alarm system, including relocation and addition of notification appliances and initiating devices, together with all associated programming coordination with Owner's fire alarm monitoring vendor;

(h) temporary power and temporary lighting for all trades throughout construction; and

(i) all testing, commissioning, labeling, as-built documentation, operation and maintenance manuals, and Owner training required by the Contract Documents.

3.2 Subcontractor shall obtain and pay for the electrical permit and all inspections required for the Subcontract Work. Contractor holds the building permit.

3.3 The following items are excluded from the Subcontract Work: utility company service fees; furnishing of low-voltage cabling and terminations for data and security systems; furnishing of ATM equipment; and patching and painting of finished surfaces, except that Subcontractor shall leave all openings it creates ready for patching by others.

3.4 Subcontractor represents that it has visited the Site, has become familiar with existing conditions, including the fact that the branch will remain partially open to members during portions of construction, and has included in its price all costs reasonably inferable from those conditions.

## ARTICLE 4 — SCHEDULE AND PROSECUTION OF THE WORK

4.1 Subcontractor shall begin the Work upon written notice to proceed from Contractor and shall prosecute it diligently, in accordance with Contractor's Project schedule, as Contractor may revise it from time to time. Time is of the essence of this Subcontract.

4.2 The Project is planned in two phases so that Owner may continue limited branch operations. Subcontractor shall perform Phase 1 Work (east side, drive-up and back-of-house) and Phase 2 Work (lobby and teller line) in the sequence shown on Contractor's phasing plan. Any shutdown of power serving occupied areas, ATMs, or security systems must be scheduled with Contractor at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance and shall be performed outside Owner's business hours unless Contractor approves otherwise in writing.

4.3 Subcontractor shall furnish sufficient qualified workers, materials, and equipment to maintain the schedule. If Subcontractor falls behind schedule for reasons within its control, it shall, at its own cost, increase its workforce, work additional shifts or overtime, or take other measures Contractor reasonably requires to recover the schedule.

4.4 Subcontractor shall attend weekly coordination meetings and shall submit a three-week look-ahead schedule each week.

4.5 If Subcontractor is delayed by an act or omission of Owner, Contractor, or another trade, or by any other cause beyond Subcontractor's control, Subcontractor shall give Contractor written notice within five (5) calendar days after the start of the delay, describing the cause and its anticipated effect. Subcontractor's sole remedy for such delay shall be an extension of time, together with any monetary compensation that Owner actually pays to Contractor on account of Subcontractor's delay claim, less Contractor's reasonable costs of pursuing it.

4.6 If the Prime Contract provides for liquidated damages and Owner assesses liquidated damages that are attributable in whole or in part to Subcontractor's delay, Subcontractor shall be liable to Contractor for the portion so attributable.

## ARTICLE 5 — SUBCONTRACTOR'S GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

5.1 Subcontractor shall at all times maintain a valid California C-10 license and all other licenses and registrations required for the Work, including registration with the California Department of Industrial Relations if applicable.

5.2 Subcontractor shall assign a competent full-time foreman who is authorized to act on Subcontractor's behalf and who shall be on Site whenever Subcontractor's forces are working. Subcontractor shall not replace the foreman without Contractor's consent, which Contractor shall not unreasonably withhold.

5.3 All electricians performing Work shall be certified in accordance with California Labor Code Section 108 et seq., or registered apprentices under appropriate supervision.

5.4 Subcontractor shall prepare and submit shop drawings, product data, and samples as required by the Contract Documents, in time to avoid delay to the Work. Approval of submittals does not relieve Subcontractor of responsibility for compliance with the Contract Documents.

5.5 Subcontractor shall coordinate its Work with all other trades, including mechanical, fire sprinkler, low-voltage, and security vendors, and shall participate in Contractor's ceiling coordination process. Subcontractor shall bear the cost of relocating its Work that conflicts with properly installed Work of others when Subcontractor installed its Work without first coordinating.

5.6 Subcontractor shall keep its work areas clean and shall remove its debris daily. If Subcontractor fails to do so within twenty-four (24) hours after Contractor's written notice, Contractor may perform the cleanup and charge the cost to Subcontractor.

5.7 Subcontractor shall maintain a current set of as-built drawings on Site, recording all deviations, concealed conduit routing, and circuit changes, and shall deliver final as-built drawings to Contractor as part of closeout.

5.8 Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable laws, codes, regulations, and ordinances, including the California Electrical Code, Title 24 energy requirements, and the Americans with Disabilities Act as applicable to devices and controls.

5.9 Subcontractor shall not employ any Lower-Tier Party for the Work without Contractor's prior written approval. Each sub-subcontract shall bind the Lower-Tier Party to Subcontractor by the terms of this Subcontract to the extent of its work.

## ARTICLE 6 — SAFETY AND SITE CONDUCT

6.1 Subcontractor is solely responsible for the safety of its employees and Lower-Tier Parties and for the safe performance of its Work. Subcontractor shall comply with the California Occupational Safety and Health Act (Cal/OSHA), Title 8 of the California Code of Regulations, Contractor's Project Safety Program, and all safety requirements of the Contract Documents and Owner.

6.2 Before starting Work, Subcontractor shall submit to Contractor its written Injury and Illness Prevention Program, a site-specific safety plan, and a Code of Safe Practices, and shall designate a competent person responsible for safety on Site.

6.3 Subcontractor shall conduct weekly toolbox safety meetings for its crews, shall keep written records of attendance and topics, and shall provide copies to Contractor on request. Subcontractor's foreman shall attend Contractor's site safety meetings.

6.4 All electrical Work shall be performed de-energized unless Contractor and Owner approve energized work in writing. Subcontractor shall implement a written lockout/tagout program meeting Title 8 requirements, shall perform voltage verification before work on any circuit, and shall provide arc-flash personal protective equipment appropriate to the equipment involved. Any energized work permitted by Contractor shall be performed under a written energized electrical work permit signed by Subcontractor's qualified person.

6.5 Subcontractor shall furnish its workers with hard hats, safety glasses, high-visibility garments, work boots, gloves, and any other personal protective equipment required by law or by Contractor's Safety Program.

6.6 Subcontractor shall comply with Contractor's fall protection requirements, including use of properly inspected ladders and mobile elevated work platforms, and shall ensure that operators of aerial lifts are trained and certified.

6.7 Because portions of the branch will remain occupied, Subcontractor shall maintain barricades, signage, and protected pathways as directed by Contractor, shall not leave open junction boxes, exposed conductors, or tripping hazards in areas accessible to Owner's staff or the public, and shall perform any noisy or disruptive Work only at times Contractor approves.

6.8 Subcontractor shall comply with Contractor's hot work permit procedures, silica exposure control requirements for core drilling and saw-cutting, and fire watch requirements.

6.9 Subcontractor shall report every injury, near miss, property damage incident, and Cal/OSHA inspection or citation involving its forces to Contractor immediately and shall submit a written incident report within twenty-four (24) hours. Subcontractor shall cooperate fully in any incident investigation.

6.10 Subcontractor shall maintain a drug- and alcohol-free workplace. Contractor may require removal from the Site of any person employed by Subcontractor or a Lower-Tier Party who violates safety rules, behaves in a disorderly or unprofessional manner, or is otherwise objectionable to Contractor or Owner, and that person shall not return without Contractor's consent.

6.11 If Subcontractor fails to correct a safety violation promptly after notice, Contractor may stop the affected Work without liability and may correct the violation at Subcontractor's expense. Subcontractor shall not be entitled to any extension of time or compensation for a stoppage caused by its safety violations. Subcontractor shall reimburse Contractor for any fines or penalties assessed against Contractor as a result of Subcontractor's violations.

6.12 Nothing in this Article shall be construed to make Contractor responsible for the safety of Subcontractor's employees or to relieve Subcontractor of its obligations under applicable law.

## ARTICLE 7 — CHANGES IN THE WORK

7.1 Contractor may, without invalidating this Subcontract, order additions, deletions, or other revisions to the Work. All such changes shall be authorized by a written Change Order or written field directive signed by Contractor's project manager. Subcontractor shall not be compensated for changed Work performed without written authorization.

7.2 When requested, Subcontractor shall submit a detailed proposal for changed Work within seven (7) calendar days, itemizing labor hours and rates, material quantities and costs, equipment, and Lower-Tier pricing. Markup for overhead and profit on changed Work shall not exceed ten percent (10%) on Subcontractor's own work and five percent (5%) on Lower-Tier work.

7.3 If Subcontractor believes that any direction, interpretation, or condition constitutes a change, it shall give written notice to Contractor within five (5) calendar days after first becoming aware of it and before performing the affected Work. Failure to give timely notice waives the claim.

7.4 Where changes arise from Owner's direction, Subcontractor's adjustment shall be limited to the amount Owner allows Contractor on Subcontractor's behalf, and Subcontractor shall be bound by Owner's determinations to the same extent as Contractor.

7.5 Pending resolution of any dispute over a change, Subcontractor shall proceed with the Work as directed.

## ARTICLE 8 — INSURANCE

8.1 Before entering the Site and for the duration of the Work, and for any longer period stated below, Subcontractor shall procure and maintain, at its own expense, the insurance described in this Article with insurers authorized to do business in California and rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best.

8.2 Commercial General Liability. Subcontractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance on an occurrence form, covering premises and operations, products and completed operations, personal and advertising injury, and contractual liability, with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, $4,000,000 general aggregate (applying per project), and $4,000,000 products-completed operations aggregate. The policy shall not contain exclusions for work performed by subcontractors, for residential or multi-unit work that would apply to the Project, or for action-over claims.

8.3 Automobile Liability. Subcontractor shall maintain business automobile liability insurance covering all owned, hired, and non-owned vehicles with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000 per accident.

8.4 Workers' Compensation and Employer's Liability. Subcontractor shall maintain workers' compensation insurance as required by the State of California, and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 each accident, $1,000,000 disease each employee, and $1,000,000 disease policy limit.

8.5 Umbrella or Excess Liability. Subcontractor shall maintain umbrella or excess liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and aggregate, following form over the general liability, automobile liability, and employer's liability coverage.

8.6 Contractor's Pollution Liability. If Subcontractor's Work disturbs or may disturb materials containing lead, PCBs (including in existing lighting ballasts), or other regulated substances, Subcontractor shall maintain contractor's pollution liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per claim. Subcontractor shall handle and dispose of all removed ballasts, lamps, and similar items in accordance with applicable hazardous waste law.

8.7 Additional Insured Status. Contractor, Owner, Architect, and any other parties Contractor reasonably designates (collectively, the "Additional Insureds") shall be named as additional insureds under the general liability, automobile liability, and umbrella or excess policies, on endorsements at least as broad as ISO forms CG 20 10 04 13 and CG 20 37 04 13 or their equivalents. Additional insured coverage for completed operations shall be maintained for not less than five (5) years after Final Completion.

8.8 Primary and Non-Contributory. Subcontractor's insurance shall be primary to and non-contributory with any insurance carried by the Additional Insureds, which shall be excess only.

8.9 Waiver of Subrogation. Subcontractor waives, and shall require its insurers to waive, all rights of subrogation against the Additional Insureds for losses covered by the insurance required by this Article, including workers' compensation, and shall obtain endorsements evidencing the waivers.

8.10 Certificates and Endorsements. Subcontractor shall deliver to Contractor, before beginning Work, certificates of insurance together with copies of all required endorsements. Contractor's acceptance of a certificate that does not comply with this Article shall not waive any requirement. Subcontractor shall provide at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Contractor of any cancellation, non-renewal, or material reduction in coverage.

8.11 Deductibles and Self-Insured Retentions. Subcontractor shall be solely responsible for all deductibles and self-insured retentions. Any self-insured retention over $25,000 requires Contractor's written approval.

8.12 Lower-Tier Insurance. Subcontractor shall require each Lower-Tier Party performing work on Site to carry insurance of the same types and with limits reasonably appropriate to its work, naming the Additional Insureds as additional insureds, and shall collect and retain certificates.

8.13 Builder's Risk. Unless the Prime Contract provides otherwise, Owner or Contractor will maintain builder's risk insurance for the Project. Subcontractor shall be responsible for any deductible attributable to loss caused by Subcontractor and for its own tools, equipment, and materials not yet incorporated into the Work.

8.14 Failure to Maintain. If Subcontractor fails to maintain any required insurance, Contractor may, without obligation, purchase that insurance and deduct the cost from amounts due Subcontractor, or may suspend Subcontractor's Work until proof of coverage is provided, without liability for delay.

8.15 No Limitation. The insurance requirements of this Article shall not limit Subcontractor's indemnity or other obligations under this Subcontract.

## ARTICLE 9 — INDEMNITY

9.1 To the fullest extent permitted by law, including California Civil Code Section 2782, Subcontractor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Contractor, Owner, Architect, and their respective officers, directors, employees, agents, and affiliates (collectively, the "Indemnitees") from and against all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' and expert fees (collectively, "Claims"), arising out of or relating to: (a) the performance of the Work by Subcontractor or any Lower-Tier Party; (b) any negligent act or omission, or willful misconduct, of Subcontractor, any Lower-Tier Party, or anyone for whose acts either of them may be liable; (c) any breach of this Subcontract by Subcontractor; (d) any violation of law, code, or regulation by Subcontractor or a Lower-Tier Party; or (e) any lien, stop payment notice, or payment bond claim asserted by a Lower-Tier Party, except to the extent caused by Contractor's failure to pay Subcontractor sums properly due.

9.2 Subcontractor's obligations under Section 9.1 shall not extend to Claims to the extent arising from the active negligence or willful misconduct of an Indemnitee, or from defects in design furnished by an Indemnitee, nor shall they apply to the extent prohibited by California Civil Code Section 2782 or 2782.05. Where a Claim arises in part from the negligence of an Indemnitee, Subcontractor's indemnity obligation shall be reduced in proportion to the Indemnitee's share of fault.

9.3 Duty to Defend. Subcontractor's duty to defend arises immediately upon tender of a Claim that alleges facts that would, if true, fall within Section 9.1, regardless of whether liability is ultimately established. Subcontractor shall defend with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnitee. As to Claims governed by Civil Code Section 2782.05, Subcontractor's defense obligation shall be performed in the manner described in that statute, including through payment of a reasonable allocated share of defense fees and costs where Subcontractor elects that option.

9.4 Employee Claims. In claims against any Indemnitee by an employee of Subcontractor or of a Lower-Tier Party, Subcontractor's indemnity obligation shall not be limited by any limitation on the amount or type of damages, compensation, or benefits payable under workers' compensation acts, disability benefit acts, or other employee benefit acts. To the extent necessary to give effect to this Section, Subcontractor waives any immunity it may have under such acts as to the Indemnitees.

9.5 Intellectual Property. Subcontractor shall defend and indemnify the Indemnitees against any Claim that materials, equipment, or methods furnished by Subcontractor infringe a patent, copyright, or other proprietary right, except where the infringing item was specified by sole source in the Contract Documents and Subcontractor had no reason to know of the infringement.

9.6 Notice and Cooperation. An Indemnitee seeking defense or indemnity shall give Subcontractor reasonably prompt written notice of the Claim, but delay in notice shall relieve Subcontractor only to the extent it is actually prejudiced. Subcontractor shall not settle any Claim in a manner that imposes liability or obligations on an Indemnitee without that Indemnitee's written consent.

9.7 Survival. The obligations of this Article shall survive completion of the Work and termination of this Subcontract and shall continue until all applicable statutes of limitation and repose have expired.

## ARTICLE 10 — WARRANTY AND CORRECTION OF WORK

10.1 Subcontractor warrants that all materials and equipment furnished under this Subcontract will be new, of good quality, and as specified, and that the Work will be free of defects in materials and workmanship and will conform to the Contract Documents.

10.2 Subcontractor shall correct, at its own cost, any Work found to be defective or nonconforming within one (1) year after Substantial Completion, or within any longer period required by the Contract Documents or by a manufacturer's warranty. Subcontractor shall begin corrective work within three (3) business days after notice, or immediately in the case of an emergency or a condition affecting Owner's branch operations, life safety systems, or security systems.

10.3 Subcontractor shall assign to Owner, through Contractor, all manufacturer warranties for equipment and materials it furnishes, and shall deliver warranty documents with the closeout submittals.

10.4 The warranty period does not limit Subcontractor's liability for latent defects or Owner's or Contractor's other remedies under law.

## ARTICLE 11 — DEFAULT, SUSPENSION, AND TERMINATION

11.1 Default. Subcontractor shall be in default if it: (a) fails to supply sufficient skilled workers or proper materials; (b) fails to maintain the schedule; (c) fails to pay Lower-Tier Parties when due; (d) disregards laws, safety requirements, or Contractor's directions; (e) fails to maintain required insurance; (f) becomes insolvent or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors; or (g) otherwise materially breaches this Subcontract.

11.2 Notice and Cure. Upon a default, Contractor may give Subcontractor written notice. If Subcontractor does not begin to cure within forty-eight (48) hours and diligently complete the cure, Contractor may, without prejudice to any other remedy, (a) supplement Subcontractor's forces and charge the cost to Subcontractor, or (b) terminate Subcontractor's right to proceed with all or part of the Work, take possession of Subcontractor's materials on Site, and complete the Work by any reasonable means. Subcontractor shall be liable for all costs of completion exceeding the unpaid balance of the Subcontract Sum, together with Contractor's reasonable overhead and attorneys' fees.

11.3 Suspension. Contractor may suspend the Work in whole or part for its convenience by written notice. If the suspension is not caused by Subcontractor, the Subcontract Sum and schedule shall be equitably adjusted to the extent Owner provides a corresponding adjustment to Contractor.

11.4 Termination for Convenience. Contractor may terminate this Subcontract for convenience on written notice. In that event, Subcontractor shall be paid for Work properly performed through the termination date, plus reasonable demobilization costs, but shall not be entitled to anticipated profit on unperformed Work. If Owner terminates the Prime Contract, Subcontractor's recovery shall not exceed the amount Owner pays Contractor on account of the Subcontract Work.

11.5 Wrongful Termination. If a termination for default is later determined to have been improper, it shall be treated as a termination for convenience under Section 11.4.

## ARTICLE 12 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 Continued Performance. Pending resolution of any dispute, Subcontractor shall proceed diligently with the Work and Contractor shall continue to make payments of undisputed amounts in accordance with this Subcontract.

12.2 Written Claim. Any claim by Subcontractor shall be submitted in writing to Contractor within twenty-one (21) calendar days after the event giving rise to it, stating the factual basis, the contract provisions relied upon, and the amount and time sought, with supporting documentation. Claims not timely submitted are waived to the fullest extent permitted by law.

12.3 Owner-Related Claims. If a dispute involves acts, omissions, or responsibilities of Owner, or seeks relief that Contractor can obtain only from Owner, Contractor may elect to present the claim to Owner on Subcontractor's behalf under the Prime Contract's claim procedures. In that case, Subcontractor shall prepare and present the claim at its own expense, shall cooperate fully, and shall be bound by the outcome of the Prime Contract dispute process to the same extent Contractor is bound. Contractor shall pay Subcontractor its share of any recovery, less Contractor's reasonable costs, within seven (7) days after Contractor receives payment from Owner. Subcontractor's claim against Contractor shall be stayed while the claim against Owner is pending.

12.4 Negotiation. The parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through meetings between their project managers. If the dispute is not resolved within fourteen (14) days, it shall be escalated to a senior officer of each party, who shall meet within an additional fourteen (14) days.

12.5 Mediation. If negotiation fails, either party may require non-binding mediation before a single mediator experienced in construction disputes, conducted in Ventura County, California. The mediator's fee shall be shared equally. Mediation shall be a condition precedent to arbitration or litigation, except where a party must act sooner to preserve lien, stop payment notice, or bond rights, or to avoid running of a limitations period.

12.6 Binding Arbitration. Any dispute not resolved by mediation shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered under the construction industry rules of a recognized arbitration provider agreed by the parties, or failing agreement, the American Arbitration Association, before a single arbitrator if the amount in controversy is $250,000 or less, and before three arbitrators otherwise. Arbitration shall take place in Ventura County, California. The arbitrator shall apply California substantive law and shall issue a reasoned written award. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction.

12.7 Joinder and Consolidation. Contractor may join Subcontractor in, or consolidate this dispute with, any arbitration or litigation between Contractor and Owner or other parties involving common questions of fact or law, and Subcontractor consents to such joinder. If the Prime Contract requires litigation rather than arbitration of disputes with Owner, Contractor may elect to have disputes under this Subcontract resolved in the same court proceeding.

12.8 Preservation of Remedies. Nothing in this Article limits either party's right to record a mechanics lien, serve a stop payment notice, make a payment bond claim, or seek provisional remedies as permitted by California law, provided that any action to enforce such rights may be stayed pending arbitration.

12.9 Attorneys' Fees. The prevailing party in any arbitration or litigation arising out of this Subcontract shall recover its reasonable attorneys' fees, expert fees, and costs.

12.10 Governing Law and Venue. This Subcontract shall be governed by the laws of the State of California. Subject to the arbitration provisions above, venue for any court proceeding shall lie in the Superior Court of California, County of Ventura.

## ARTICLE 13 — SUBCONTRACT SUM, PAYMENT, AND RETENTION

13.1 Subcontract Sum. Contractor shall pay Subcontractor, for the full and complete performance of the Work, the sum of Three Hundred Eighty-Six Thousand Dollars ($386,000.00), subject to additions and deductions by Change Order. The Subcontract Sum is allocated as shown in the Exhibit — Schedule of Values.

13.2 Applications for Payment. On or before the 20th day of each month, Subcontractor shall submit to Contractor an application for payment covering Work completed through the end of that month, itemized according to the Schedule of Values, together with updated as-built drawings, certified payroll records if required, and the lien waivers required by Section 13.8. Contractor may adjust the application to reflect its assessment of the Work actually completed and then include it in Contractor's application to Owner.

13.3 Stored Materials. Contractor will not pay for materials or equipment that are stored on or off the Site and not yet incorporated into the Work; applications for payment shall include only installed Work.

13.4 Time of Payment. Payment to Subcontractor is conditioned on Contractor's receipt of payment from Owner for the Work covered by the application. Contractor shall pay Subcontractor the amount due within seven (7) days after Contractor receives payment from Owner for that Work. The parties have agreed to this pay-when-paid timing provision in writing by signing this Subcontract. This Section governs the timing of payment and does not make Owner's payment a condition to Contractor's ultimate obligation to pay for Work properly performed.

13.5 Retention. Contractor shall withhold retention of five percent (5%) from each progress payment to Subcontractor.

13.6 Release of Retention. Retention shall become due upon Final Completion of the Subcontract Work and Contractor's receipt of retention from Owner. Contractor shall pay the retention to Subcontractor within ten (10) days after both conditions have been satisfied, subject to Subcontractor's delivery of all closeout documents, warranties, and final lien waivers required by this Subcontract.

13.7 Withholding and Setoff. In addition to retention, Contractor may withhold from any payment amounts reasonably necessary to protect Contractor against loss on account of: (a) defective or nonconforming Work that has not been corrected, in an amount up to one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the reasonable estimated cost of correction; (b) claims, liens, or stop payment notices filed or reasonably threatened by Lower-Tier Parties; (c) Subcontractor's failure to make payments to Lower-Tier Parties; (d) damage to the work of others caused by Subcontractor; or (e) reasonable evidence that the Work cannot be completed for the unpaid balance. Contractor may set off against any amount due Subcontractor under this Subcontract all backcharges, costs, and damages for which Subcontractor is liable to Contractor, including costs of cleanup, supplementation, or completion under Articles 5 and 11. Contractor shall give Subcontractor written notice of each withholding or backcharge stating its basis and amount. Amounts withheld for defective Work shall be released with the next progress payment after the defect is corrected and accepted.

13.8 Lien Waivers. As a condition of each progress payment, Subcontractor shall deliver with its application a conditional waiver and release on progress payment covering the amount requested, and within ten (10) days after receipt of each payment shall deliver an unconditional waiver and release on progress payment covering the amount received. As a condition of final payment, Subcontractor shall deliver a conditional waiver and release on final payment, followed upon receipt of payment by an unconditional waiver and release on final payment. All waivers shall be executed on the statutory forms set forth in California Civil Code Sections 8132, 8134, 8136, and 8138, as applicable, and no other form shall be accepted. Subcontractor shall also obtain and deliver, with each application, conditional and unconditional waivers on the same statutory forms from each Lower-Tier Party that furnished labor, materials, or equipment for the Work covered by the application or by prior payments.

13.9 Payment Not Acceptance. No payment, including final payment, shall constitute acceptance of defective or nonconforming Work or waive any of Contractor's rights.

13.10 Final Payment. Final payment of the balance of the Subcontract Sum, other than retention governed by Section 13.6, shall be made when Subcontractor has completed the Work, delivered all closeout documents, and satisfied all conditions of this Article, and in accordance with the timing stated in Section 13.4.

13.11 Prompt Payment to Lower Tiers. Subcontractor shall pay each Lower-Tier Party, from each payment received, the amounts due it within the time required by California law, and shall hold payments received in trust for that purpose.
